Sibility Flow

Grants for Novingos

Funding and knowledge for small, independent non-violence initiatives

What it’s about

Independent non-violence NGOs (or novingo) often do the hardest, least visible work. Most don’t have a fundraising team, a grant writer on staff, or spare months to fill out applications. Usually it's just a few people doing something that matters where they live.

That's why we decided that in 2026-2027 we give $500,000. Every person on our team either worked at a novingo or alongside one. So, we know for certain that independent teams working on non-violence lack support. Right now we're in a position to change that. It’s just part of a big, long effort to reduce violence in the world.

What’s included

The grant consists of two parts: money and learning programs.

Part one
Money
$500,000

Sibility Initiative distributes $500,000 among the winners of the grant. The money is meant to cover whatever your team actually needs, whether that's day-to-day costs, a specific project, or something else entirely.

Part two
Learning

Sibility Initiative provides your organisation with a set of educational tools and practical courses. For instance, workshops on community management, low-budget marketing, basic accounting for NGOs, and more.

You can request money only, education only, or both. The amount of support may differ. We don't promise everyone the same sum. Depending on the applications and tasks, someone gets more, someone less. No reporting is needed. We assume you know better than we do what it should be spent on.

Who it’s for

This grant supports non-violence NGOs (novingo). To apply, your organisation has to:

Work on non-violence and be independent. Competitors cannot be a part of the government or a large structure that is able to fund you without us.

Operate at a small, local, grassroots level, rather than as a big international network with large budgets.

Be based in the US, the UK, or any EU country.

Apply for a grant

We want to know who you are, what you do, where you work, how much money you need, and whether you want the educational part too. If you've applied for grants before, you'll easily fill this form out with no trouble. If you haven't, don't worry — we made the form simple and straightforward on purpose.

Questions and feedback

We know that submitting an application may be a hassle (and a little nerve-racking). But we have tried to make the process as intuitive as possible. Sorry if it is not perfect; we'll get better next time! And if we can help in any way at all, please write to us at hello@sibility.org.

We'll get back to you within a few days — in writing first, and then, if you wish, we can set up a call.
